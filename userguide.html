User Guide

Everything you need to get the most out of SmartCart Shopper — from your first scan to Pro features.

🚀 Getting Started

SmartCart works straight out of the box — no account, no sign-in. Open the app and you land on your shopping list, ready to scan.

Android App
Install from Google Play. Tap the scan button (🔲) in the bottom nav to start your first scan.
🌐

Web version
💶
Default currency
Defaults to EUR €. Change anytime in About → Currency & Live FX. 20 currencies supported.

📷 Scanning Barcodes

Tap the barcode button in the bottom navigation to open the scanner. The app tries three scanning methods in order, falling back automatically if needed.

1
Point at a barcode
Hold your phone 10–20 cm from the barcode. The scanner works with EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, UPC-E, Code-128, Code-39, and ITF formats.
2
Product is looked up
SmartCart checks in order: your Price Book → Offline Database (if downloaded) → Open Food Facts → Open Beauty Facts → UPC Item DB.
3
Result sheet slides up
Product name, brand and category appear. Enter the price, adjust quantity, then tap Add to Cart. The camera stays live so you can keep scanning.
4
Price is remembered
Once added, the price is saved to your Price Book against the current store name. Next visit, your remembered price pre-fills automatically.
💡 Scan from manual entry: Tap the ☞ Scan button next to "Add Item" in the manual entry modal to fill the form via barcode without leaving the modal.
⚠ Free limit: 15 scans per day per store on the free tier. The FAB badge counts down your remaining scans. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited.

🛒 Shopping List

The List tab is your main workspace during a shop. Items are grouped by category, sorted by name by default.

1
Add items
Scan a barcode — or tap + (bottom right) to add manually. Manual entry supports name, brand, category, price and quantity.
2
Check off items
Tap any item to toggle it checked/unchecked. Checked items are dimmed — the total only counts unchecked items so you know what's left.
3
Edit or remove
Long-press or tap the ✏️ icon to edit price, quantity or category. Swipe or tap 🗑 to remove an item.
4
Complete the session
Tap Done Shopping (tick icon) to save the trip to History. All prices are recorded to your Price Book.
💡 Quick-add from Price Book: In the manual entry modal, expand "Quick Add from Price Book" to pick a saved item — name, brand, price and category pre-fill instantly.

📒 Price Book

The Price Book (Prices tab) records every product you've ever scanned, with prices per store. It's how SmartCart knows your best deal.

1
Automatic recording
Every time you add a scanned item with a price, it's saved automatically — no action needed.
2
Best price highlight
Each entry shows the lowest price found across all stores with a green indicator. Tap any entry to see full price history per store.
3
Edit or delete
Tap an entry to edit the product name or brand. Swipe or tap 🗑 to remove it entirely from your price book.
Free limit: Up to 30 entries in the Price Book on the free tier. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited entries.

📋 Session History

Every completed shopping trip is saved as a full receipt in the History tab.

👁
View receipt
See the full itemised list, store name, date, total and budget comparison.
🔁
Reuse list
Tap "Reuse List" to load all items from a previous trip into your current shopping list.
🖨️
Print receipt PRO
Print a clean formatted receipt for any trip — great for expense reports.
🗑
Delete session
Remove any session from history. This does not affect your Price Book entries.
Free limit: The 3 most recent sessions are visible on the free tier. Older sessions are locked behind a Pro upgrade.

🗄️ Offline Product Database PRO

Download 4.2 million products covering food, beauty and household goods. Once downloaded, barcode lookups work with zero internet.

1
Go to About tab
Scroll to the Offline Database section. Tap Download (~460 MB). A Pro subscription is required.
2
Download completes

3
Offline lookup active

Fallback: smartcart-db.markeumir.xyz — served via Cloudflare if GitHub is slow.

💾 Backup & Restore

Your data lives on-device. Use Export/Import to back it up, move it between devices, or keep a copy on your computer.

↓
Export JSON
About tab → Backup & Restore → Export JSON Backup. Choose "Save to device" (free) or "Share to other apps" (Pro). File is named smartcart-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json.
↑
Import JSON
Tap Import Backup, choose your .json file. Import merges Price Book entries and replaces your current list and sessions. A warning is shown before overwriting.
What's included in a backup: Shopping list, price book (all entries), session history, budget settings. App settings (currency, Pro status) are not included.

❓ FAQ

Why does the scanner open twice?
This was fixed in v6. If you see two scanners, update to the latest version. Tapping X or Cancel on the native scanner now returns to the app silently — the fallback only activates on a real hardware error.
My barcode isn't found — what do I do?
Try downloading the Offline Database (Pro) for 4.2M products. If it's still not found, add it manually using the + button. Your manual entry will be remembered for next time.
Can I use SmartCart without internet?
Yes — the app works offline for Price Book lookups and manual entry. Download the Offline Database (Pro) to also look up barcodes without internet.
Is my data backed up to the cloud?
No — all data is stored on-device only. Use Export JSON to create your own backup. See Backup & Restore above.
I lost my Pro unlock code — what do I do?
Your Device ID is shown in About → SmartCart Pro → Unlock. Contact the developer with your Device ID and proof of purchase and a new code will be issued.
Camera doesn't open on the web version
Camera requires HTTPS. Opening the HTML file directly via file:// will show the HTTPS Required screen — serve it from a web server or use the Android APK instead.
