Data Deletion

SmartCart Shopper stores all your data locally on your device. You are in full control — there is no account to delete and no server to contact for your shopping data. This page explains every way to clear your data, and what to do if you have a Pro purchase record held by RevenueCat.

1. No Account — Nothing to Delete on Our End

SmartCart Shopper requires no registration, login, or account of any kind. We hold no personal data on any server — your name, email address, or any identifier is never collected by us.

All app data — your shopping lists, price book, session history, budget settings, and currency preferences — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. Deleting it from your device deletes it completely and permanently. There is no cloud copy to request removal of.

2. Delete Your Data Instantly (No Email Needed)

Choose whichever method works best for you. Both are immediate and irreversible.

INSTANT · IN-APP
Option A — Clear All Data inside SmartCart

This removes all shopping data while keeping your app settings and Pro status intact.

1
Open SmartCart Shopper on your device.
2
Tap the About tab at the bottom of the screen.
3
Select Backup & Restore.
4
Tap Clear All Data and confirm when prompted.

✅ All shopping lists, session history, price book entries, and budget data are erased immediately.

INSTANT · SYSTEM
Option B — Clear via Android App Settings

This wipes everything — shopping data, settings, and cached files — as if the app was freshly installed.

1
Go to your device Settings.
2
Tap Apps (or Application Manager on some devices).
3
Find and tap SmartCart Shopper.
4
Tap Storage, then tap Clear Data.
INSTANT · UNINSTALL
Option C — Uninstall the App

Uninstalling SmartCart Shopper removes the app and all locally stored data from your device completely. Long-press the app icon and select Uninstall, or go to Settings → Apps → SmartCart Shopper → Uninstall.

If you uninstall and reinstall later, all previous shopping data will be gone. Export a backup first if you want to restore your data.

3. Pro Purchase & RevenueCat Entitlement Data

If you purchased SmartCart Pro through Google Play, your purchase entitlement is verified via RevenueCat. RevenueCat may hold a minimal purchase record linked to your Google Play App User ID or purchase email.

Note: SmartCart itself holds no copy of this data. The record exists only on RevenueCat's servers for the purpose of restoring your Pro access across reinstalls. Clearing app data (Methods A, B, C above) does not remove this RevenueCat record.

To request deletion of your RevenueCat purchase entitlement record, email us using the template in Section 4 below. We will forward your deletion request to RevenueCat on your behalf within 14 days.

Please be aware that deleting this record means your Pro purchase can no longer be restored. If you reinstall SmartCart Shopper, you will not be able to recover your Pro access without repurchasing.

For full details on what RevenueCat stores and your rights, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Email Deletion Request (for RevenueCat Data)

If you purchased Pro and want your RevenueCat entitlement record deleted, send us an email. Please include the following information so we can locate and action your request accurately:

  • App name: SmartCart Shopper
  • Package ID: com.mirodigitaltools.smartcartshopper
  • RevenueCat App User ID (if known — visible in the app under About → App Info) and/or
  • Google Play purchase email address associated with the Pro purchase

Use the template below as a starting point:

To mygmail@markeumir.xyz Subject Data Deletion Request — SmartCart Shopper Body Hello,

I am requesting deletion of my data associated with the following:

• App name: SmartCart Shopper
• Package ID: com.mirodigitaltools.smartcartshopper
• RevenueCat App User ID: [your ID here, if known]
• Google Play purchase email: [the email used for purchase]

Please delete my purchase entitlement record held by RevenueCat.

I understand this will prevent Pro access from being restored in future reinstalls.

Thank you.
Where to find your RevenueCat App User ID: Open SmartCart Shopper → About tab → scroll to App Info. It will be listed there if applicable.

5. Processing Timeline & GDPR Rights

We aim to action all data deletion requests within 14 days of receiving a complete request with sufficient identifying information. You will receive a confirmation email once the request has been processed.

EU / GDPR users: You have the right to erasure of personal data under Article 17 of the GDPR. If you believe your request has not been handled correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Germany, this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (BlnBDI).

Note: Because SmartCart Shopper collects no personal data directly, the only data subject to a formal GDPR Article 17 request is the RevenueCat purchase entitlement record described in Section 3. All other app data is on your device and under your direct control.
