Features

Everything you need at the checkout

Built for real shopping trips — fast, offline-ready, and completely private.

📷
Smart Barcode Scanner
3-tier scanner — native ML Kit, WebView BarcodeDetector, and html5-qrcode fallback. EAN-13, UPC-A, Code-128 and more. Torch support included.
💰
Price Memory
Remembers every product price per store. Alerts you when the same item is cheaper elsewhere and highlights your best deal automatically.
🛒
Shopping List & Budget
Add items by scan or manually. Set a budget and watch your running total in real time. Progress bar turns amber at 80%, red when over budget.
🗄️
4.2M Offline Products
Download a 460 MB SQLite database with 4.2 million products covering food, beauty and household. Lookup works with zero internet.
📋
Session History
Every completed trip saved as a full receipt. View totals, budget comparisons, category breakdowns. Reuse any list for your next trip.
💱
Currency & Live FX
20 currencies supported. Default EUR. Live exchange rates via frankfurter.app — cached hourly. Display-only conversion keeps your stored data safe.
🔒
100% Private
All data stored on-device only. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics SDKs, no advertising. Camera used exclusively for barcode scanning.
🖨️
Print Receipts (Pro)
Generate a clean printed receipt for any shopping session. Includes itemised list, quantities, store name, budget and total.
