Privacy Policy

SmartCart Shopper is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy. This policy explains exactly what data is collected, where it goes, and who can see it. The short answer: everything stays on your device.

1. Who We Are

SmartCart Shopper is developed and maintained by Mark Eumir Turingan, operating under the brand MiRo Digital Tools, based in Berlin, Germany.

This privacy policy applies to all versions of SmartCart Shopper — the Google Play version, the External/web version, and any future releases.

2. What Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit or store any personal data on external servers.

3. Camera Permission

SmartCart requests camera access for one purpose only: scanning barcodes.

  • The camera is activated only when you explicitly tap the scan button.
  • No photos, video or screenshots are taken, saved or transmitted.
  • The camera feed is processed entirely on-device, frame by frame, to detect barcode patterns.
  • The camera stops as soon as you close the scanner or navigate away.
Camera permission can be revoked at any time in your device Settings → Apps → SmartCart → Permissions.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Version)

Pro upgrades on the Google Play version are processed by Google Play Billing via RevenueCat. SmartCart does not handle payment information directly.

  • Payment data is handled entirely by Google — see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat receives a purchase token to verify entitlement — no personal data beyond what Google provides. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • SmartCart stores only a local Pro status flag (true/false) on your device after a verified purchase.

6. Device ID (External Version Only)

The external version of SmartCart uses a locally generated Device ID to produce unlock codes for Pro activation.

  • The Device ID is a randomly generated value created on first launch and stored only in your device's localStorage.
  • It is not derived from any hardware identifier, IMEI, MAC address or personal data.
  • The Device ID is only transmitted when you choose to share it with the developer to request a Pro unlock code.
  • The developer uses it solely to generate your unique unlock code — it is not stored, sold or shared.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you are in complete control:

  • Clear app data: Device Settings → Apps → SmartCart → Storage → Clear Data. This removes everything instantly.
  • Clear specific data: About tab → Backup & Restore → Clear All Data removes your shopping data while keeping app settings.
  • Uninstalling SmartCart removes all locally stored data.

We hold no copies of your data on any server — deletion on your device is complete and final.

8. Children's Privacy

SmartCart Shopper does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU under GDPR). As no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

9. GDPR (EU Users)

SmartCart is developed in Berlin, Germany and is designed to be GDPR-compliant by default. Because we collect no personal data, there is no data processing activity to declare under Articles 13/14 of the GDPR. Specifically:

  • No lawful basis for processing is required — no processing occurs.
  • No Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is needed with third-party API providers, as only non-personal barcode numbers are transmitted.
  • You have full rights of access, rectification and erasure over your data — exercised entirely through the app itself.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use of SmartCart after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

Given the no-data-collection nature of this app, significant changes are unlikely.
